## Measurement contract

All performance and load measurements remain on Railway, not on the user's workstation. Local work is compilation and small deterministic correctness/protocol tests only. Keep the existing database 2-CPU/2-GB and driver 2-CPU/1-GB caps, unchanged fsync guarantees, source-qualified configuration and independent raw/aggregate oracles. Do not touch the original service or erase old evidence. Each workload has a hard deadline; stop owned compute promptly between campaigns rather than leaving an unbounded benchmark running.

Keep every v6 query pattern and both automatic-tier/columnar stages. Do not substitute a single favorable scan for an overall win. For the final claim, use at least three fresh matched trials, counterbalance backend order, record all latency samples and account for Timescale refresh/ANALYZE. Seek at least 10% throughput improvement for both initial durable ingest and durable-plus-fresh readiness, and at least 10% per-pattern p95 latency improvement; an inconclusive/noisy cell remains inconclusive, not a pass. Require the fixed 20k mixed target to complete without drops/errors/ambiguity and without hiding tails. Report median run ratios and individual trials, not only the best run. The paired queue does not establish either backend's standalone saturation frontier; separate bounded standalone sweeps are needed for that claim.

Additional larger/colder/lifecycle workloads are separately named, not replacements for Timescale's strong cases. Varve's automatic object-store restore/independently retained history can be an integration advantage over the tested self-hosted Timescale configuration; do not claim that every Timescale product/deployment lacks tiering, retention, continuous aggregates, or external backup tooling. Equal durability scope must remain explicit: local fsync is not synchronous S3 replication or HA.

## Residency law

A user clarification is central: data must not eagerly trickle through every layer. Durability, checkpoint progress, serving representation and eviction are separate decisions. A locally fsynced append does not authorize immediate columnarization or archival. Hot memory is a first-class serving layer, not a waiting room for Parquet. A durable checkpoint must not be treated as a reason to discard a useful resident representation automatically.

Movement needs an explicit age/lifecycle policy, measured capacity pressure, or an identified execution/storage benefit. Inactivity alone is not a reason to churn unchanged state. Expiration deadlines still apply exactly. Audit the existing five-second flush policy and 100k hot-row rollover separately from hard process-memory caps; preserve the original benchmark profile, and label any separately residency-tuned profile honestly rather than claiming unchanged internal settings. No unbounded RAM retention or weakened WAL durability is authorized.

## Design boundaries

Optimization must remove representation conversion, unnecessary selected data, repeated whole-state computation, or serialized I/O; do not just increase budgets or rename work as background. Keep one durable commit order without requiring every read to wait for all preparation. Typed small-input SQL is bounded materialization, not zero-copy Arrow. Derived pages are not out-of-core merely because the control root is small. A safe WAL redesign must distinguish uncommitted crash tails from corrupted acknowledged history under the supported contract and include restore/shipping/GC before activation.
